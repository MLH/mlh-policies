# HackSaigon Disciplinary Action Policy

To ensure that HackSaigon is a safe and welcoming environment, HackSaigon and its organizers reserve the right to refuse participation in the event to any individual. This document outlines common reasons an individual could be removed from or refused entry to HackSaigon, the procedure for notifying the individuals involved of suspensions or bans, and the appeals process.

## Actions We May Take

If someone violates the HackSaigon Code of Conduct, Community Values, event rules, or any other official HackSaigon policy, we will take action to resolve the issue. Because HackSaigon is fully virtual, these actions apply across every online space used for the event, including the chat server, voice and video calls, livestreams, team channels, and project submission platforms. Actions typically include, in order of severity:

- **Warning** the person to stop their behavior, and making clear that any further reports will result in further sanctions
- **Requiring no contact:** directing the person to avoid any interaction with the affected participant for the remainder of the event, including direct messages, mentions, and shared voice or video channels
- **Muting or restricting** the person's access to chat channels, voice or video calls, or other event spaces, and removing offending content
- **Ending volunteer, mentor, judge, or organizer responsibilities** and removing any related roles, permissions, and access
- **Removing the person from the event**, including all event channels and platforms, and disqualifying them or their team where appropriate
- **Banning the person from future HackSaigon events**, either indefinitely or for a set period of time

Participants who are removed or disqualified are not eligible for prizes, swag, or other rewards.

## Notification Procedure

Participants who are issued a Temporary Suspension or Permanent Ban will be notified in writing by email. The email will include:

- The exact cause for the Temporary Suspension or Permanent Ban
- The length of the Suspension (Bans are permanent)
- Instructions for how to appeal

If an individual attempts to register for or join a HackSaigon event that takes place during their Temporary Suspension or Permanent Ban, including by using a different email address, username, or account, HackSaigon will use the following procedure:

- The participant will be reminded in writing by email that they are not allowed to participate in the event.
- The event organizers and moderators will be notified in writing that the individual is currently suspended or banned and should not be allowed to participate. Their registration will be cancelled, and their access to event servers, calls, and platforms will be blocked.

In the interest of privacy, HackSaigon will not disclose the reason for a Temporary Suspension or Permanent Ban to other organizers, moderators, or participants unless the individual requests that we do so.

## Appeals

Participants may appeal a Temporary Suspension or Permanent Ban by submitting a written request by email to **contact@hacksaigon.org** with "\[Appeal\]" in the subject line, attaching any relevant evidence, such as screenshots, message logs, or a written explanation. Appeals should be submitted within 7 days of the date of the notification email.

Appeals will be reviewed by members of the HackSaigon team who were not involved in the original decision where possible. We will respond in writing, and the outcome of an appeal is final.

## Changes to This Policy

HackSaigon reserves the right to revise, make exceptions to, or otherwise amend these policies in whole or in part. If you have any questions regarding these policies, please contact HackSaigon by email at **contact@hacksaigon.org**.
