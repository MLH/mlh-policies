# HackSaigon Privacy Policy

HackSaigon ("HackSaigon", "us", or "we") respects the privacy of the people who visit our website and take part in our fully virtual hackathon. The HackSaigon website, together with the event platforms we use to run HackSaigon (such as our chat server, video calls, and project submission site), will collectively be known as the "Services". This Privacy Policy explains our policies and practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of any Personal Information (defined below) and Anonymous Information (defined below) you submit to us or that we collect through the Services.

"Personal Information" is information about you that is personally identifiable to you, such as your name, email address, or username, as well as other non-public information that is associated with them. "Anonymous Information" is information that is not associated with or linked to your Personal Information and does not allow the identification of individual people. We collect and use Personal Information and Anonymous Information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## User Consent

When you submit Personal Information through the Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy and you expressly consent to the handling of your Personal Information as described in it. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

## Information We Collect

We may collect Personal Information that you submit to us voluntarily. We collect this information when you register, create or edit a profile, join a team, answer survey questions, submit a project, enter contests, use features of the Services, join our chat server or calls, or contact us. The types of information you may choose to provide include, without limitation:

- Name, username, email address, and other contact information
- School or university, student status, age or year of study, country, and time zone
- Your preferences, skills, interests, and any other information you choose to share in your registration or profile
- Information you submit as part of your hack, such as project descriptions, code repository links, demos, and videos
- Content you post in event spaces, such as chat messages, voice or video participation, and screen shares
- Shipping details, if you win a prize that needs to be delivered to you

Because HackSaigon is virtual, we or our third-party service providers may also automatically gather information about your devices and use of the Services, such as device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, approximate location, and usage information.

If you choose to join through a third-party platform, such as a chat or video service, that platform may share certain information with us, such as your username, user ID, and profile picture, in line with its own privacy practices.

## Use of Information

We may use your Personal Information for the following general purposes:

- To run the event, including processing registrations, forming teams, communicating with you, providing support, judging projects, and delivering prizes
- To respond to your inquiries
- To address administrative needs and communications
- To keep the event safe, enforce our Code of Conduct, and carry out our Disciplinary Action Policy
- To improve our event and services
- To send you updates, surveys, and notices about changes to the event or our policies
- To send you newsletters and promotional communications, if you have chosen to receive them

We use Anonymous Information, such as the pages you have viewed or how participants used the event platforms, to analyze usage patterns so that we can improve the event, enhance the participant experience, and gather broad demographic information for aggregate use. We may disclose Anonymous Information to third parties, such as sponsors and partners, in our discretion.

## Information Sharing

Except as stated in this Privacy Policy, we do not sell, trade, or rent your Personal Information to third parties. If you provide Personal Information for a certain reason, we will use it in connection with that reason. For example, if you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide to answer your question or resolve your problem and will reply to the address you wrote from.

**Service providers.** We may share your Personal Information with contractors and third-party service providers who help us run the Services and the event, such as email, hosting, video, chat, analytics, and project submission platforms. We share information with them only so they can provide those services to us. We are not responsible for how those providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

**Sponsors and partners.** If you choose to opt in, for example by checking a box at registration, we may share your name, contact details, and relevant profile information with sponsors and partners so they can offer you opportunities such as jobs or internships. We do not control the privacy practices of sponsors and partners. We may also share aggregate, non-personal information with them, such as the number of participants or their countries of residence.

**Mentors, judges, and organizers.** Information you submit about your project, and your name and team details, will be visible to mentors, judges, volunteers, and organizers to the extent needed to run and evaluate the event.

**Public content.** Messages you post in public channels, project submissions, demos, and winners' names may be visible to other participants and, where the event's rules say so, to the public (for example on a project gallery or winners list). Please do not share information in public spaces that you do not want others to see.

**Contests and special promotions.** If you enter a contest, drawing, or prize, you agree to its official rules, which may allow us to use your name and project in announcements, marketing, or winners lists, except where prohibited by law. Personal Information may be disclosed to third parties or the public in connection with administering the contest, such as for winner selection or prize fulfillment.

**Business changes.** If another organization takes over or acquires HackSaigon or its assets, it will have the Personal Information we have collected and will take on the rights and obligations in this Privacy Policy.

**Legal and safety reasons.** We may share Personal Information if we believe it is necessary to:

- Investigate, prevent, or take action about illegal activity, suspected fraud, situations involving possible threats to the safety of any person, or violations of our Code of Conduct, event rules, or other policies, or as otherwise required by law
- Respond to legal process such as a subpoena or court order, or to establish, exercise, or defend our legal rights
- Restrict or prevent anyone from using the Services, including through hacking, disruption, or defacement

Cameras and microphones are optional, and you may choose not to appear on camera or to leave a recorded session. Participants may not record or capture other people's video, audio, or private messages without their consent.

You have options regarding the collection, use, and sharing of your Personal Information. Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, update, or delete the Personal Information we hold about you, or object to or restrict certain uses of it, by emailing **contact@hacksaigon.org**. ## Children's Information

To use the Services, you must be 13 years of age or older. If you are between 13 and 18 years of age (or are otherwise a minor where you live), you must have your parent or legal guardian's permission to take part in HackSaigon. If we learn that we have collected Personal Information from someone younger than 13, we will delete it. You must not use the Services if you are barred or otherwise legally prohibited from doing so under the laws of the country where you live or from which you access the Services, or if you have previously been banned from HackSaigon. While using the Services, you agree to abide by the HackSaigon Code of Conduct.

If you use the Services on behalf of someone else, you represent that you are authorized to do so and that your use is that person's acceptance of this Policy.
